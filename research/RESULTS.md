# Proximity Validation Results

Results from executing the evaluation pipeline in [evaluate.py](proximity_validation/evaluate.py) and grid sweeps in [run_experiments.py](proximity_validation/run_experiments.py) on synthetic datasets generated by [generate.py](proximity_validation/generate.py). Full raw outputs are stored in [experiments.csv](results/experiments.csv), [summary.md](results/summary.md), and [baseline_output.txt](results/baseline_output.txt).

---

## 1. Research Questions & Baseline Motivation

1. **Question 1:** Do category/taxonomy and semantic proximity predict emotional similarity between places better than geographic distance alone?
2. **Question 2:** Can `composite_proximity` weights (`geo=0.3, cat=0.4, ling=0.15, cult=0.15`) be learned from emotional similarity data?
3. **Question 3:** How much CHIME emotional variation is specific to an individual place ("this particular church") rather than shared across its type ("any church")?

As shown in [baseline_output.txt](results/baseline_output.txt), pooling visits by `place_type` assigns both `anc_church_recovery` (mean `Hope=0.867`, `volatility=0.035`) and `sea_church_grief` (mean `Hope=0.200`, `volatility=0.041`) the identical `church` signature (`Hope=0.533`, `volatility=0.261`), removing `church` from `detect_recovery_anchors(threshold=0.6)` even though one specific church is a strong anchor and the other is not.

## 5. What Each Result Means — and Does NOT Mean

- **`M1 > M0` (`0.316` vs. `-0.030` at `geo_corr=0.0`) and `cat` ablation (`-0.0895` Spearman):**
  - *Means:* When places of the same type are spread across cities, taxonomy similarity predicts synthetic emotional similarity while geographic distance does not, and Ridge (`M4`) / NNLS (`M5`) rely primarily on `category_tree`.
  - *Does NOT mean:* Does not prove real-world geographic proximity is irrelevant; in real cities, neighborhood environment or co-located stressors could carry genuine geographic signal not modeled here.
- **`M0` rises from `-0.030` to `0.062` when `geo_corr=1.0`:**
  - *Means:* Spatial clustering of place types makes geographic proximity pick up category-driven emotional similarity even with zero true geographic effect in the emotion model. Any study claiming geography predicts emotion must test for spatial confounding by venue type.
  - *Does NOT mean:* Does not provide the exact magnitude of confounding to expect in real cities (which have more than 4 cities and different neighborhood scales than `max_distance_km=500.0`).
- **`M2` (`semantic_name`) positive correlation (`0.097`–`0.204`):**
  - *Means:* Templated names (`"St. Mary's Church"`, `"Harborview Church"`) contain the category noun literally, so char 3-gram TF-IDF acts as a noisy proxy for `category_tree`.
  - *Does NOT mean:* Does not show that place names predict emotion independently of place type.
- **`M4` / `M5` beat repo `M3` (`0.165`/`0.214` vs. `0.056`) while trailing univariate `M1` (`0.279`):**
  - *Means:* Fixed hand-set weights (`M3`) degrade predictions by assigning `0.60` combined weight to uninformative dimensions (`geo=0.3, ling=0.15, cult=0.15`). Learning weights suppresses those dimensions, though fitting 5 correlated weights from ~44 places adds finite-sample variance compared to using `category_tree` alone when only category carries signal.
  - *Does NOT mean:* Does not mean `ling` and `cult` are uninformative in real populations; our generator planted no direct `language` or `cultural_tags` effect.
- **`Var(Place|Type)` rises from `0.036` (`place_str=0.0`) to `0.323` (`place_str=1.0`), dropping `M1` from `0.343` to `0.201`:**
  - *Means:* Type-level signatures break down as individual physical places acquire distinct emotional profiles, directly illustrating why DREAMS needs place-instance (`place_id`) tracking in addition to `place_type`. Note also that unadjusted ANOVA sum-of-squares allocates ~`0.03–0.08` to `Var(Place|Type)` even at `place_strength=0.0` due to finite visit counts per place.
  - *Does NOT mean:* Does not tell us what fraction of variance in real DREAMS users comes from physical places versus types (`0.036` and `0.323` reflect chosen generator settings).

## 6. Limits

1. **All data is synthetic:** Every place, timestamp, and CHIME distribution comes from a seeded parametric model. Recovering planted category/group/place structure confirms our estimators and cross-validation do not leak or fail mathematically; **it provides zero empirical evidence about real human emotions or recovery journeys.**
2. **Shared generative assumptions:** Both the generator and `category_tree_similarity` use the same 3-level taxonomy tree, and synthetic names leak category nouns into `semantic_name`.
3. **Approximate fold bootstrap:** `evaluate.py` bootstraps across the `M` already-fitted LPO fold scores for speed; because LPO folds share overlapping pairs, 95% CIs are slightly narrower than full bootstrap re-fitting.
4. **What real data would change:** Testing these models on real or consented recovery memories (timestamps, venue/GPS clusters, and human-annotated or validated CHIME scores collected under **IRB approval**) would replace planted noise with unmodeled confounding (user mobility biases, seasonality, missing locations, unequal visit counts, noisy OSM categories, and genuine linguistic/cultural effects) and reveal which dimensions and shrinkage weights actually generalize.
